Privacy Policy for Mani CRM

Effective Date: July 31, 2026 | Last Updated: July 31, 2026

At Mani CRM ("we", "our", "us"), accessible from https://mani-crm.co.in, we respect your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Name, email address, phone number, business name when you register.
  • Customer Data You Upload: Customer profiles, leads, tasks, notes, follow-ups that you enter into Mani CRM. You are the controller of this data.
  • Usage Data: Device information, log data, IP address, app version, and interactions for analytics and crash reporting.
  • Authentication Data: If you use Google Login, we receive your name, email, and profile picture from Google.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the Mani CRM service
  • To authenticate users and secure accounts
  • To improve app performance and user experience
  • To send important service updates, reminders, and support messages
  • To comply with legal obligations

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with:

  • Cloud Service Providers: For hosting (e.g., AWS / Google Cloud / Firebase)
  • Authentication Providers: Google, if you use Google Sign-In
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely on encrypted cloud servers. We use industry-standard security measures. While we strive to protect your data, no system is 100% secure.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account data as long as your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us. CRM data you entered can be exported and deleted from within the app.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact us at support@mani-crm.co.in

7. Children's Privacy

Mani CRM is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Third-Party Services

Our app may use third-party services like Google Authentication, Firebase Analytics, and Crashlytics. Their use is governed by their respective privacy policies.

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
