Data Deletion Instructions - Mani CRM

At Mani CRM, we respect your right to delete your data.

How to request data deletion:

Option 1: In-App Deletion (Recommended)

  • Login to Mani CRM App
  • Go to Settings > Account > Delete Account
  • Your account and all associated data will be permanently deleted within 7 days.

Option 2: Email Request

Send an email to support@mani-crm.co.in from your registered email with subject "Request Data Deletion". Include your registered phone number / email. We will delete your data within 7 business days and confirm via email.

What data is deleted?

  • Your account profile (name, email, phone)
  • All CRM data you created (customers, leads, tasks, notes)
  • Login tokens and session data

Data Retention

After deletion, no personal data is retained except as required for legal compliance (e.g., billing records for 1 year).
