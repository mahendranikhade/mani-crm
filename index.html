What is Mani CRM?

Mani CRM is a cloud-based customer relationship management solution designed to help businesses organize customer data, track leads, manage follow-ups, and improve team productivity without complexity.

Key Features

Customer Management

Centralized customer profiles with contact details, history, and notes.

Lead Tracking

Track leads from inquiry to conversion with clear status and ownership.

Task & Follow-ups

Never miss a follow-up with reminders and activity tracking.

Multi-Business Ready

Designed to support multiple offices, showrooms, and teams.

Secure & Scalable

Built on modern cloud infrastructure with security and scalability in mind.

Simple & Fast

Clean UI focused on speed, usability, and real business workflows.
